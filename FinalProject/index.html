Game Instructions

Welcome to Maze of Ice and Flame – a 2D adventure where only the right moves ensure your survival and victory.

  • Movement: Use A/D (or Left/Right Arrow keys / On-Screen D-Pad) to move horizontally.
  • Jumping: Press the W key, Up Arrow, or the On-Screen jump button to leap.
  • Dangerous Regions: Each hero has its own dangerous region. Stepping into the hazard zone of the wrong character results in death.
  • Switches: Some mechanisms must be triggered by a specific hero elemental type to progress.
  • Star Collection: Accumulate stars hidden throughout the maze structures to boost your ranking score.
