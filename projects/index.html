How to Enable WebGPU

WebGPU is the next generation of web graphics, but it may require a quick setting change:

Chrome/Edge: Go to chrome://flags/#enable-unsafe-webgpu and set it to Enabled.
Windows: Ensure Hardware Acceleration is ON in Browser Settings > System.
Safari: Go to Settings > Advanced > Feature Flags > Check WebGPU.
